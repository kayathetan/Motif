## What it does

Every industry has already told you what works in short-form video — hook
timing, cut rhythm, CTA placement, the shape of the reveal. It's just
scattered across thousands of videos nobody has time to reverse-engineer by
hand.

Motif does that reverse-engineering for you:

- **📊 See the market first** — dominant format, hook timing, CTA and
  content-type mix, and the structural benchmark top performers hit in your
  industry, measured from real published content.
- **🎬 Get a brief your team can shoot from** — hooks, a timestamped outline,
  pacing, shot list, on-screen text, and the CTA line, all traced back to
  what the industry measurably rewards. Not advice — a production document.
- **🔎 Every brief shows its work** — how many real videos backed it, how
  targeted the match was, and links to the actual reference videos it drew
  on. Not something a plain LLM prompt could produce.

1. **Tell us your industry, audience, and goal.**
2. **Motif shows what's already winning** in that industry — pulled from
   patterns in real top-performing content, not a generic trend list.
3. **Get a scene-by-scene brief**, generated by matching your campaign
   against that evidence, with the real reference videos it drew on.

## Under the hood

| Layer | Stack |
|---|---|
| Frontend | React + Vite, Clerk auth |
| Backend | FastAPI, per-user rate limiting |
| Data | Supabase (Postgres + pgvector) |
| Intelligence | OpenAI GPT-4o (multimodal pattern extraction + embeddings) |

```
frontend/   React + Vite client
backend/    FastAPI server — brief generation & niche intelligence
pipeline/   builds the pattern library that both of the above read from
```

<details>
<summary><b>How the pattern library gets built (and why it's small right now)</b></summary>

`pipeline/build_library.py` is the ingestion path: given a video URL, it
pulls metadata + transcript, computes timing signals in code, classifies the
video's niche against already-verified examples, and — only on a confident
match — samples frames and calls GPT-4o to extract a structural pattern.

Right now that list of URLs is small and hand-curated rather than
auto-discovered: every video that passes classification costs a download
plus several GPT-4o calls plus embeddings, and that adds up fast with no
revenue behind it yet.

In production this becomes a scheduled worker instead of a script someone
runs by hand: continuously discovering candidates per niche via the YouTube
Data API, and feeding every one through this same confidence-gated
classifier — discovery just changes where the URL comes from, not how a
video gets accepted.

</details>
